# Trend Breakout prohibited-variant register

Status: `FROZEN_FAMILY_LEVEL_PROHIBITION`

The following are prohibited as new candidates, amendments, renamed strategies, or research branches:

- Donchian-20 breakout-entry variants;
- breakout-continuation confirmation variants;
- breakout-boundary retest confirmation variants;
- tuning or removing the EMA-distance rule using family evidence;
- tuning the retest window;
- tuning the spread fraction or absolute spread ceiling;
- tuning the ATR stop multiple;
- changing Donchian entry or exit periods based on V14 or V23 performance;
- adding session, volatility, direction, distance, timing, or other filters selected from losing-trade analysis;
- combining V1, V2, V3.0, or V3.1 rules under a new name;
- creating Trend Breakout V4, V3.2, or another version of this family.

The register does not prohibit maintaining reusable bot infrastructure. It prohibits further strategy hypotheses derived from this closed family and its observed failures.
