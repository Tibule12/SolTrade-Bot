## Reconciliation and replay limits

The Sep 30 event stream overlaps the Sep 8 broker history on 26 closed position IDs. All 26 EA whole-position cash values match broker profit, commission, swap and fee to the cent (combined −$268.41). All 60 EA exits match the fresh full broker export to the cent. The 34 EA exits after the Sep 8 snapshot total **−$4,074.83**; fresh broker cash flow between that snapshot and the Sep 30 visual check is also **−$4,074.83**. Thus $98,531.43 − $4,074.83 = **$94,456.60**, exactly the displayed Sep 30 balance. The seven exits on Sep 9–10 total +$2,050.17, taking the Sep 8 balance to the documented Sep 11 flat $100,581.60 baseline; the Sep 11 loss of $1,001.96 gives the Sep 13 flat $99,579.64 baseline. The 26 exits after Sep 13 total −$5,123.04. All of these anchors now reconcile independently against timed broker deals.

After the Sep 30 visual check, the carried GER40 408708254 closed for **+$862.42** including its +$5.91 swap, US100 409016573 closed for **−$661.04** including +$43.62 swap, and GER40 409382791 closed for **−$945.80**. Their net **−$744.42** bridges the Sep 30 balance to the Oct 1 export balance of **$93,712.18**. The Sep 30 GER40 partial is the eleventh two-close position in the fresh broker export; its first close occurred after the visual check. These three late outcomes are not present in the retained Sep 30 EA event CSV.

The fresh deal ledger supports an exact chronological realized-balance replay, including each entry commission, partial exit, final close, swap and fee. The ten earlier partial exits mean a whole-position EA `EXIT` amount cannot be booked only at its final exit timestamp. The Sep 24 carried XAUUSD trade, for example, realized a broker partial profit of $471.96 at 10:23:20 UTC; its $5.52 opening commission had already been charged, while EA allocated $469.20 net to the bank. True intratrade equity and drawdown still need historical bid/ask or equity marks; a deal ledger alone only fixes realized cash. EA `PARTIAL_EXIT_DEAL` top-level `entry`/`stop` fields are cached scoring fields, not broker fills. The Sep 30 report also documents misleading `banked_cash` on unbanked losers; use broker deals and explicit `partial_banking` to classify banks.
