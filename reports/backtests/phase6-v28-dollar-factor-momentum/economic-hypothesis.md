# V28 dollar-factor momentum hypothesis

Written before any local V28 signal count or return is viewed.

Shaojun Zhang, “Dissecting Currency Momentum,” *Journal of Financial Economics* 144 (2022), 154-173, DOI `10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.05.035`, reports that currency momentum is concentrated in autocorrelated carry and dollar factors rather than idiosyncratic currency returns. A factor-momentum investor buys a factor after a positive formation return and sells it after a negative formation return; the paper reports positive results for one-, three-, and twelve-month formation periods.

V28 tests the one-month dollar factor only. The dollar factor is the equal-weight foreign-currency return versus USD across seven majors. If its prior rebalance-to-rebalance return is positive, V28 buys all seven foreign currencies for the next month; if negative, it sells all seven. This expresses a single systematic dollar-regime position while diversifying pair-specific noise.

The rule is falsified unless both 2025 and pre-seal 2026 Development partitions pass every frozen sample, cost, execution, drawdown, concentration, and consistency gate.
