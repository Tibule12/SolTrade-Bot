# Next-strategy research policy

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_NEXT_STRATEGY_DESIGN`

1. The next candidate must use a fundamentally different market hypothesis from Donchian trend breakout or breakout-retest continuation.
2. It must not be constructed by modifying V1, V2, V3.0, or V3.1 losing-trade rules.
3. Its economic or market-structure rationale must be written before signal counts or P&L are viewed.
4. Exactly one complete candidate strategy may be frozen for the next research cycle.
5. Competing variants, parameter sweeps, automatic optimization, and hidden optimization are prohibited.
6. All strategy parameters, sample gates, performance gates, and acceptance rules must be committed before profitability testing.
7. All data before `2026-08-01 00:00:00` may be used only as Development evidence.
8. The unopened reserve may be assigned only after the new candidate passes its frozen Development screen.
9. Phase 7, demo-forward testing, and live trading remain unauthorized.
10. Failure of the next fully tested strategy requires an explicit project-level review before another strategy family is created.

V24 does not propose, design, compare, or authorize the next strategy.
