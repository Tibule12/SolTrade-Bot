**Disposition: `FP_BASELINE_CASH_RECONCILED__THREE_ACCOUNT_COUNTERFACTUALS_UNRESOLVED`.** I built a chronological, orderless deal-ledger engine and ran it against a fresh read-only FP broker export. It reproduces every provided FP deal, position close and balance change, including one-time partial closes. I also ran recorded-score, current-flow, first-move, timing, opposite-side boundary, break-even quote-path and per-loss diagnostics. **None of the 23 experimental arms has an honest final account balance yet.** The required opposite-side trading lifetimes, native opposite stops, broker-valid alternative fills and FXIFY broker histories are absent; the [account × arm matrix](account-arm-matrix.csv) leaves those results null rather than turning partial quote paths into invented profits.

This is a material result about the bot, not a claim that it is fixed. In the comparable FP manager cohort, **14 initial structural-stop losses alone closed at −$13,185.97 / −13.7445R**. The same cohort also had winners, leaving **26 trades at −$5,123.04 / −5.09624R** from the September 13 flat baseline to the September 30 balance snapshot. The current production entry score did not distinguish those outcomes reliably; it also did **not** show reliable negative information that would justify flipping it.

## Score polarity and entry location

On the **26 recorded FP admissions** after September 13, production admission score versus realized net R has Pearson **+0.161** and Spearman **+0.054**. The fixed 2.5-point bands and rank deciles, with wins, full stops, confirmed Bank1R and price-peak milestones, are in the [score diagnostic](fp-score-diagnostic.json). `conflict=false` on all 26, so that flag separated nothing. `no_trade_score` was 12 on 24 entries and 36 on two losses; the latter sample is too small to establish a reliable filter. M1 opposed 13 entries (four wins, nine losses), was non-directional on 12 (six and six), and agreed on one loser. These states overlap across winners and losers. The FXIFY mixed-version score correlations are negative, but only four frozen-manager trades per account exist and all four lost; the accounts share many signals. **There is no demonstrated positive or inverse production-score rule here.** [Recorded FP score rows](fp-score-trades.csv) · [FXIFY score bins](fxify-event-diagnostic.md).

## Opposite direction and management tests: measured boundaries, not fabricated returns

The 29 baseline-lifetime exports contain **215,215 ticks**, with zero export errors and zero decreasing timestamps. Three paths have market-close/reopen gaps exceeding 30 minutes, so no quote is interpolated through those gaps. [Tick continuity receipt](fp-quote-continuity.json).

For the 29 FP quote paths, I evaluated **actual opposite-side bid/ask** at the original entry and mirrored the *initial distance* only. Before each original position's actual exit, the inverted quote path reached its mirrored initial stop nine times, +1R twice, and was still open in 14 cases; four entry anchors/stops were unresolved. Normal direction reached its original initial stop 15 times, +1R nine, was right-censored once, and unresolved four. **A +1R crossing is not a final trade profit.** The 14 inverted positions still open at the original exit need *later* quotes, a native opposite-side structural stop, Bank1R/runner simulation, execution charges and propagated account risk. This diagnostic cannot say inversion wins, loses or changes final balance. [Boundary rows](fp-inversion-initial-boundaries.csv) · [coverage](fp-inversion-initial-boundaries.json).

On the same observed baseline quote paths, a separately frozen **+0.5 price-R then entry-price floor** armed 16 times and the quote later crossed that floor 12 times. Four crossings preceded an eventual actual Bank1R; seven preceded an eventual profitable close. Of 11 actual two-close positions, the post-bank runner quote later crossed entry price before the actual final exit in five. That exposes both possible protection and premature-exit risk. **Entry price is not cash breakeven**, and historical broker stop/freeze acceptance, alternate fills, slippage, cost allocation and later account sizing are not known. M1–M4 manager, +0.5R BE and ownership arms therefore have no exact cash result. [Per-position quote paths](fp-management-quote-paths.csv) · [summary and rule text](fp-management-quote-summary.json).

The flip, dual-side probe, score swap across *all* opportunities and hindsight inversion oracle likewise have **no final R or balance** in this delivery. The source archive contains some scanner results, but not a joined three-account, executable quote-and-broker-state path for every alternative trade. The matrix supplies one explicit missing dependency per arm. The [replay engine](../../../tools/three_account_replay/engine.py) refuses to present fixed-fill accounting adjustments as broker execution; its synthetic tests cover interleaved partial closes, commission, position volume, account isolation, order of same-millisecond deals, clock offset, missing risk/equity and censoring. Its open-risk columns use recorded *initial* risk prorated with volume; they are not contemporaneous portfolio risk because the full stop-change timeline is unavailable.

## Isolation, validation and reproducibility

All new code is in `tools/three_account_*`, `tools/run_three_account_baseline.py`, `tools/build_three_account_tick_requests.py`, and `tools/three_account_replay/`. It reads preserved CSVs or the isolated research export. **No research tool has an order-send, order-modify or position-close API.** The local history probe compiled and ran with terminal live trading disabled; both broker and tick export receipts say `read_only=true`, `live_trading_allowed=false`, and report zero export errors. The research made **zero real orders or position changes**. The FP autonomous EA continued trading during the audit; its subsequent broker deals are included in the baseline and are not research orders. I did not issue a VPS command or modify FP source, binary, preset, stop or entry permissions; FXIFY pause files, collector, tracker and evaluator were untouched. The last preserved September 30 FXIFY evidence still shows disabled startup entry settings; this local pass cannot independently attest to *current* FXIFY broker exposure or protected VPS hashes. [Orderless isolation receipt](isolation-receipt.json).

The **25 regression and live-evidence checks pass**. They include exact FP balance and 60/60 EA EXIT cash reconciliation, both FXIFY flat-anchor bridges, read-only export completion, zero order API, causal pre-entry tick cutoff, quote-side entry matching, mirrored-boundary right censoring and prevention of false BE cash claims. [Test receipt](test-results.json) · [source/evidence SHA-256 manifest](files.json) · [exact account-arm matrix](account-arm-matrix.csv).

The immediate engineering conclusion is narrow but concrete: the observed cash destruction is led by **pre-bank FP structural-stop losses**, while ownership and post-bank management remain real operational concerns whose **incremental economic cost is unresolved**. The current score, simple M1 alignment, first tick and opposite-flow labels do not furnish a validated reversible rule. The baseline ledger is strong enough to identify where actual cash was lost; it is **not** sufficient to invent the three-account counterfactual balances requested above.
