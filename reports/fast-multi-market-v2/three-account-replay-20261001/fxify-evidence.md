## Order, quote, history, and replay coverage

I found no FXIFY account-specific broker `deals.csv`, `orders.csv`, `history-status.csv`, account transaction/deposit ledger, equity time series, or independent broker ticket export in the preserved repository/VPS output. The `reports/fast-multi-market-v2/{all-symbol-audit,entry-location-audit,false-rejection}-20260908/broker/` exports identify **FP 7404213**, so they cannot serve as FXIFY broker history. FXIFY EA `EXIT.detail` provides booked-net claims and position IDs, but independent deal, partial-fill, commission, balance, and cash-flow verification requires fresh broker history for each login.

FXIFY scan/structure files are `ops/forexvps/remote-output/fxify-scan-audit/f{10,100}-{scan-history-v5,structure-telemetry-v6}-2026090{1,2,3}.csv`. They record roughly ten-second, per-symbol candidate observations, executable entry/stop estimates, spread and structure fields, not tick-level bid/ask paths or continuous account equity. Additional bounded captures are `ops/forexvps/remote-output/live-cross-account-audit-20260904-0514.zip` (September 3 late session and September 4 through about 03:13 UTC) and `trade-review-paths-20260907.zip` (September 7 01:30–03:20 UTC). No retained FXIFY scan paths cover the September 14–15 trades. `ops/forexvps/remote-output/v202-vps-ticks/manifest.json` identifies the four August `.tkc` cache files as **FP 7404213** data, so they are not September FXIFY quote history.

The [prior FXIFY banking report](../fxify-banking-counterfactual-20260910/report.md) explicitly censored the changed-holding paths at missing September 4/7 post-entry quotes: only 9/14 10K and 8/13 100K independent banking opportunities resolved, with further portfolio censoring. It also notes sampled rather than tick observations, missing account-inception history, unverified account-specific provider rules/reset time, and unmodeled broker stop/freeze, margin, slippage, swap, and dynamic correlation effects. Consequently the preserved data supports a matched **actual** September 13–16 balance bridge and bounded earlier opportunity screens; it cannot establish an exact full-history challenge pass, intratick daily drawdown, or native counterfactual portfolio outcome for either FXIFY login. A defensible exact replay needs fresh per-login broker deals/orders and balance operations, current broker exposure, account-specific rule/reset evidence, and continuous FXIFY bid/ask history over every changed holding window.
